## Game Components
- **Draw Pile**: Face-down stack of cards players draw from
- **Discard Pile**: Face-up stack where played cards are placed
- **Player Hands**: Each player's current set of cards
- **Element Indicators**: Shows current element in play (for Void cards)

## Setup
1. Shuffle the deck and deal 7 cards to each player
2. Place the remaining cards face down as the draw pile
3. Flip the top card to start the discard pile
4. If the first card is a special card, follow its effect immediately

## Game Flow
1. On your turn, play a card that matches the current element, number, or play a special card
2. If you can't or don't want to play, draw a card from the draw pile
3. Special cards have immediate effects when played
4. Play continues clockwise unless reversed

## Card Types

### Number Cards (1-9)
- Match by color, number, or element
- Point value equals face value

### Special Cards (20 points each)
1. **Skip**: Next player loses their turn
2. **Reverse**: Changes direction of play
3. **Stack**: Removes matching/weak elements from discard pile
4. **Strike**: Forces next player to draw 2 cards (see [Strike Mechanics](#strike-card-mechanics))
5. **Void** (50 points): Transfers discard pile and changes color (see [Void Mechanics](#void-card-mechanics))

## Elemental System
Elements follow a rock-paper-scissors style hierarchy:

```
🔥 Fire > 🌱 Plant > ⚡ Thunder > 💧 Water > 🔥 Fire
```

- A stronger element can counter a weaker one
- Elements of equal strength cancel out
- Void cards can match any element when played

## Special Cards Mechanics

### Strike Card Mechanics
When a Strike card is played:
1. The next player must either:
   - Draw 2 cards (first from discard pile if available, then draw pile)
   - Play a card of the same element
   - Play a Strike card of a stronger element (see hierarchy above)
   - Play a Void card (cancels effect and allows color change)

2. If countering with a Strike:
   - The effect passes to the next player
   - The new Strike must be of a stronger element
   - The chain continues until someone can't counter

3. If forced to draw:
   - First take cards from the discard pile (if any)
   - Then take remaining from the draw pile
   - If draw pile is empty, shuffle discard pile (except top card) to form new draw pile

### Void Card Mechanics
- When played, the player:
  1. Chooses a new element
  2. The entire discard pile (except the Void) is given to the next player
  3. The Void card stays as the new top card
- Can be played on any turn
- Counters any Strike card when played in response

## Winning the Game
The game ends when:
1. A player plays their last card (they win immediately), or
2. The draw pile is exhausted and no more moves are possible
   - Player with the lowest total points wins
   - In case of tie, the player who would have gone next wins

## Game Modes

### Classic
- 5-second turn limit
- 2 penalty cards for timeouts
- Standard rules apply

### Time Bank
- Each player has a 90-second personal timer
- Timer counts down during their turn
- Players are eliminated when their time runs out
- Last player remaining wins

### Blitz
- 3-second turn limit
- Fast-paced gameplay
- 3 penalty cards for timeouts

## Strategy Tips
1. **Elemental Advantage**: Remember the element hierarchy for countering Strikes
2. **Void Timing**: Save Void cards for critical moments or to counter powerful Strikes
3. **Strike Chains**: Be cautious when starting a Strike chain - it might come back to you
4. **Discard Pile Awareness**: Watch the discard pile for potential Stack plays
5. **Hand Management**: Balance between keeping high-value cards and maintaining play options
6. **Time Management**: In Time Bank mode, make quicker decisions with a low time bank

## Penalties
- **Invalid Play**: Must draw 2 penalty cards
- **Timeout**: Draw 1 penalty card + any pending Strike cards
- **Disconnection**: Auto-draw until hand size matches other players

## Advanced Rules
- When the draw pile is exhausted, the discard pile (except top card) is shuffled to form a new draw pile
- If a player can't draw when required (empty draw pile), they skip their draw
- Players must say "BoltIs" when they have one card left (house rule, optional)
